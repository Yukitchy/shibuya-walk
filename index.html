Course A · The walk your clients ask for

Shibuya & Harajuku

The crossing, the rooftop park, the Nintendo and Pokémon shops, then a back street to Harajuku and a forest walk at the end. You see it the way a guest would.

The day

  1. 11:00
    Hachiko statue, Shibuya stationWe meet at the dog.
  2. 11:10
    Scramble crossingCross it once, then watch it from the rooftop of MAGNET by SHIBUYA109 across the street.
  3. 11:40
    Miyashita ParkA park on the roof of a mall. Skate park, climbing wall, and Shibuya Yokocho food alley underneath.
  4. 12:15
    Shibuya PARCO, 6th floorNintendo TOKYO, Pokémon Center Shibuya, CAPCOM Store and the Jump Shop on one floor.
  5. 13:15
    LunchSee the three picks below.
  6. 14:00
    Cat Street to HarajukuA 15-minute walk on a quiet street of small shops.
  7. 14:30
    Takeshita streetThe crowded one. Crepes, character shops, photo booths.
  8. 15:00
    Meiji JinguForest walk to the shrine and back. We finish at Harajuku station around 15:45.

The route

Everything on foot. Shibuya station → PARCO 10 min. PARCO → Harajuku 15 min. Harajuku station has the JR Yamanote line back. Open the route in Google Maps ↗

Good for No tickets. Easy to cut short. This is the standard Shibuya day, so it is useful to have walked it yourself.

Keep in mind Takeshita street is crowded after noon on Saturday. The PARCO shops are busy but the queues move.

Course B · The view, then the small-town side

Shibuya & Shimokitazawa

Shibuya from 230 metres up, then one short train ride to Shimokitazawa, where Tokyo people go on weekends. Vintage shops, small theatres, curry.

The day

  1. 11:00
    Hachiko statue, Shibuya stationWe meet at the dog.
  2. 11:15
    Shibuya SkyOpen rooftop on top of Scramble Square. About 45 minutes. I book the timed ticket.
  3. 12:15
    Scramble crossing and Shibuya StreamCross at street level, then a short walk along the river side of the station.
  4. 12:45
    Keio Inokashira line to ShimokitazawaTwo stops, 4 minutes.
  5. 13:00
    LunchCurry is the local dish here. See the picks below.
  6. 14:00
    Shimokitazawa streetsVintage shops, record shops and Bonus Track, a row of small shops on the old rail line.
  7. 15:00
    Coffee, and doneWe finish at Shimokitazawa station. Shibuya is 4 minutes, Shinjuku 10.

The route

Shibuya on foot. Shibuya → Shimokitazawa 4 min on the Keio Inokashira line. Shimokitazawa → Shibuya or Shinjuku 4 to 10 min. Open the route in Google Maps ↗

Where we eat

Keep in mind Shibuya Sky needs a timed ticket, about 2,500 yen, booked online. Windy on the roof.

Course C · Downhill to the river

Shibuya, Daikanyama & Nakameguro

Start in the noise, then walk 20 minutes downhill into the quiet part: the Daikanyama bookshop, then the Meguro river and its cafés. Easy pace, good for talking.

The day

  1. 11:00
    Hachiko statue, Shibuya stationWe meet at the dog.
  2. 11:15
    Scramble crossing and Shibuya StreamCross once, then follow the river side of the station.
  3. 11:45
    Walk to Daikanyama20 minutes, mostly downhill, through a residential area.
  4. 12:10
    Daikanyama T-Site and Log RoadTsutaya Books, three buildings of books and a lounge. Log Road is a garden path on an old rail line with a brewery.
  5. 13:00
    LunchSee the picks below.
  6. 14:15
    Walk to Nakameguro15 minutes.
  7. 14:30
    Meguro riverThe cherry-tree street. Small shops and cafés on both banks.
  8. 15:15
    Starbucks Reserve Roastery, and doneThe big four-floor one by the river. We finish at Nakameguro station.

The route

Everything on foot, about 4 km in total. Shibuya → Daikanyama 20 min. Daikanyama → Nakameguro 15 min. Nakameguro station has the Hibiya and Toyoko lines. Open the route in Google Maps ↗

Where we eat

Ivy PlaceDaikanyama · brunchPancakes, salads and pasta in the T-Site garden. Beer flight and a proper lunch.Open in Google Maps ↗Onibus Coffee NakameguroNakameguro · coffeeSmall roaster next to the tracks. Standing room, good beans.Open in Google Maps ↗

Good for The least crowded option. Flat and easy. Good if you want time to talk business.

Keep in mind The Meguro river cherry trees are green in October, not pink. Ivy Place can be a 30-minute wait on Saturday.
