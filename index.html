Downhill to the river

Shibuya, Daikanyama & Nakameguro

Start in the noise, then walk 20 minutes downhill into the quiet part: the Daikanyama bookshop, then the Meguro river and its cafés. Easy pace, good for talking.

The day

  1. 13:00
    Hachiko statue, Shibuya stationWe meet at the dog.
  2. 13:15
    Scramble crossing and Shibuya StreamCross once, then follow the river side of the station.
  3. 13:45
    Walk to Daikanyama20 minutes, mostly downhill, through a residential area.
  4. 14:10
    Daikanyama T-Site and Log RoadTsutaya Books, three buildings of books and a lounge. Log Road is a garden path on an old rail line with a brewery.
  5. 15:00
    Late lunch or coffeeSee the picks below. Ivy Place serves food all afternoon.
  6. 15:50
    Walk to Nakameguro15 minutes.
  7. 16:05
    Meguro riverThe cherry-tree street. Small shops and cafés on both banks.
  8. 16:45
    Starbucks Reserve Roastery, and doneThe big four-floor one by the river. We finish at Nakameguro station around 17:15.

The route

Everything on foot, about 4 km in total. Shibuya → Daikanyama 20 min. Daikanyama → Nakameguro 15 min. Nakameguro station has the Hibiya and Toyoko lines. Open the route in Google Maps ↗
